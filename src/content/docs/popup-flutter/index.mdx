`deepdots_popups` is a Flutter plugin that shows Deepdots popups and surveys in your Android and iOS app. It wraps the native Deepdots Popup SDK: targeting, triggers, sessions, analytics and the survey UI all run natively, and the plugin exposes them to Dart and wires in Flutter's navigation and app lifecycle for you.

## What it does

- Popups are configured in Deepdots and delivered to the app at runtime: no release needed to launch or change a campaign.
- Triggers by screen, time on screen, scroll, exit, custom event and click.
- Segmentation by screen, language and user, with cooldowns handled by Deepdots.
- The survey renders inside a native popup, on top of your Flutter UI.
- A Dart stream of popup events (`popup_shown`, `popup_clicked`, `survey_completed`) for your own analytics or UI.
- Behavioral analytics (screens, sessions, engagement, custom events) sent to your Deepdots workspace.

## What the plugin does for you

- **Navigation:** add `DeepdotsNavigatorObserver` to your app and every named route is reported to the SDK.
- **Lifecycle:** the plugin opens and closes sessions when the app goes to the foreground or background. There is nothing to call.
- **Scroll:** wrap a screen in `DeepdotsScrollListener` and scroll triggers work.
